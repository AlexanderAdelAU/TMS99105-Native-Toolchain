COMMONLIBRARY BASELINE
======================

The files under common/ are copied from the supplied top-level CommonLibrary
baseline and are treated as authoritative inputs for SBC LINK99 development.
The older nested CommonLibrary/CommonLibrary copy is intentionally omitted.

  common/Includes/   canonical public headers, including rel99.h and
                     rel99_format.h
  common/rel99/      GETREL.c, PUTREL.c, SEEREL.c
  common/cfunctions/ shared Small-C helper implementations

Do not create LINK99-private copies of REL item values.  New linker code must
include/use the common definitions so GETREL, PUTREL, SEEREL and LINK99 remain
one format family.
