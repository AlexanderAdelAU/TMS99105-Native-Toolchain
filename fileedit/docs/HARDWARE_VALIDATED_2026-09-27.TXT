Status
------
ACCEPTED HARDWARE-VALIDATED PERFORMANCE BUILD.

Hardware result
---------------
On the target SBC, inserting characters works correctly and there is no
appreciable insertion delay even with large files.

Implementation retained
-----------------------
* Known-good FILEEDIT99 baseline remains the foundation.
* Only insertion is accelerated.
* A small leaf routine moves the bank-0 portion of the suffix directly with
  MOVB; it performs no mapping calls, no nested calls, and no stack changes.
* Bank 1, heap, shell RAM, boundary bytes, and deletion remain on the original
  known-good ebget()/ebput() path.
* Existing EBPUT ABI, CR/LF handling, and current-line redraw fixes are retained.
